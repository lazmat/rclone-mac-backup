Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026.

This OAuth client is for personal use by one person. It is not offered to other users.

Data

  • The app (rclone on the owner’s Mac) accesses Google Drive only to upload and download that owner’s own backup files.
  • Tokens stay on the owner’s Mac in rclone’s local config. They are not sent to a third-party server.
  • No analytics, ads, or sharing of Google user data with anyone else.
